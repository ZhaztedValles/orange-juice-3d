# Facts: Broughton Suspension Bridge, Salford, 12 April 1831; 74 men of the 60th Rifle Corps marching four abreast; the bridge vibrated in time with their steps; one badly forged bolt snapped; nobody killed; the army then ordered troops to break step on bridges.
