# Facts: Denby Dale, a village near Huddersfield in West Yorkshire, England, has baked giant pies to mark great occasions since 1788 (the first for King George III's recovery) (Wikipedia; Foods of England). For Queen Victoria's Golden Jubilee in August 1887 the villagers baked a pie of about one and a half tons that included game (Foods of England); preparing it took so long, in summer heat, that it went bad (Wikipedia; Yorkshire Post): at the first cut a stench of rotten meat came out, and the pie had to be emptied into a pit and buried in quicklime (Yorkshire Post; Wikipedia; Foods of England). In September 1887 the village baked a replacement, without game, called the Resurrection Pie, and it was a success (Wikipedia; Foods of England).
Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denby_Dale, https://www.yorkshirepost.co.uk/heritage-and-retro/heritage/how-denby-dale-invented-the-giant-meat-pie-3012776, https://foodsofengland.info/denbydalepie.html

In eighteen eighty-seven, a Yorkshire village baked a pie so big and so proud that it ended up in a grave.
Since seventeen eighty-eight, Denby Dale had marked great royal days by baking giant pies.
That summer Queen Victoria had ruled for fifty years, so they planned their greatest pie of all.
They built an enormous dish and packed it with meat and game, about a ton and a half of pie.
Making it took days, in the warm air of late summer.
At last it was baked, and the crowds arrived with plates, forks and very big appetites.
The first knife went in.
And out came a smell so bad that people stepped back.
The meat inside had gone rotten.
You see, a giant pie is like a huge pot of stew, the middle stays warm for hours after the edges cool, and warm meat is a feast for germs.
So the great Jubilee pie was emptied into a pit and buried in quicklime, like a monster locked away for good.
Denby Dale could have given up on pies forever.
Instead, that September, they baked a new one, with no game at all.
It was a huge success, and everyone went home full.
And the name they gave it?
The Resurrection Pie, because in Denby Dale, even a pie can rise from the grave.
